About Aya

Nothing here is an accident. Every part of Aya means something.

Aya Recovery & Wellness is building Roots & Wings, a recovery residence for women in Jacksonville, serving Duval, Clay, and St. Johns Counties. Our doors open in Fall 2027.

Valencia Johnson founded Aya Recovery & Wellness to build a home where women in recovery are met with belonging. She builds the way she thinks: meaning first.

She holds a bachelor's degree in Nonprofit Leadership and Management from Arizona State University, with a minor in Social Work. As an undergraduate she worked as a research assistant in ASU's School of Social Work, and that work still shapes how Aya measures progress: by the strengths a woman builds, not only by what she leaves behind.

Today she works in healthcare and builds Aya in the hours around it. She is working toward Florida's Certified Recovery Peer Specialist credential.

Every name and symbol at Aya has a story behind it. Nothing was added for decoration.

The name and the mark

A fern that grows in hard places.

In the Akan Adinkra tradition of Ghana, the aya fern stands for endurance and resourcefulness.

Our fern has exactly eight branch pairs, one for each level of the Aya Journey. It rises from a seed, with its roots in plain view.

Roots below. Wings above. That is the whole idea.

Our philosophy

Three ideas hold the house up.

We grow as individuals.

Each woman moves at her own pace, on her own path. Roots & Wings is MAT-inclusive and spiritually non-prescriptive. Her recovery is hers.

We thrive in community.

Healing happens in the company of other women. Our model draws on Malidoma Patrice Somé's work on communal healing. A home, not a facility.

We share responsibility.

For the house, for one another, and for the neighborhood we join. What lets one woman grow is held by all of us.

Walk with us while we build.

Roots & Wings opens in Fall 2027. Until then, follow the build, or tell us about the women you serve.
