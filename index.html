Our mission

Aya Recovery & Wellness provides a safe home where each woman in substance use recovery moves through eight levels of witnessed transformation across her health, family, work, and purpose. She returns to community with roots and wings.

Aya means fern. To the Akan people of Ghana, it is an Adinkra symbol of endurance and resourcefulness, a plant that grows in hard places.

While we build

Roots & Wings is on its way

We are preparing a home in Jacksonville for women in recovery, and our doors open in Fall 2027. Until then, we are listening to the providers, families, and neighbors who will shape it.

Need help before we open?

Roots & Wings opens in Fall 2027. If you or someone you love needs support now, you don't have to wait for us.

  • In an emergency or a possible overdoseCall 911.
  • In crisisCall or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, any time. That includes a substance use crisis.
  • Treatment and support near youSAMHSA's National Helpline, 1-800-662-4357, is free and open 24/7 in English and Spanish. You can also search FindTreatment.gov.
  • A recovery residence open nowSearch FARR's directory of certified housing across Florida.
