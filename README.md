# Aya Recovery & Wellness website

The public site for ayarecoverywellness.org. Netlify publishes this folder
automatically whenever `main` changes.

## Do not move or rename

- `assets/aya-fern.png` is loaded by the Aya email signatures. If it moves or
  is renamed, the fern disappears from every signature already in use.

## Keep in sync

- "Opening Fall 2027" appears on the home page and in both email signatures.
  If the opening date changes, update the site, the signatures and the
  Current Facts sheet together.
