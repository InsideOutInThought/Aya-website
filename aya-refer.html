At a glance

What to know before you refer

Who we serve
Adult women (18+) committed to recovery, with at least 60 days substance-free at admission. Not there yet? Call anyway, and we'll time the referral with you.
Level of support
Built to NARR Level II standards: peer-led and peer-monitored. Not 24/7 staffed, and not a clinical program. She lives here while she does the clinical work.
MAT
Welcome and protected. Prescribed medications, including MAT, are never a barrier to admission.
Legal status
Justice-involved women are welcome, including women in drug court, on probation, on parole, or on pretrial release. With her consent, we keep her officer or court team informed.
Clinical care
Continuing outpatient care, IOP, and prescriber appointments are required and coordinated with her providers.
Fees
One published rate for every woman, shared when you call. Deferred payment is available on request.
Location
Shared with referring partners and accepted applicants. That's standard safety practice for a women's residence.
Not a fit
Women who need detox or a higher level of care. Medical cannabis is prohibited on the premises under Florida law, and active use is a disqualifying admission criterion. That's a legal distinction, not a moral one.

The referral journey

From first call to move-in

  1. 1

    Call or email

    Reach us at (904) 372-3723 or by email. We respond within one business day.

  2. 2

    A conversation

    A short conversation with her, and with you. We look at her strengths, not just her risks.

  3. 3

    Conditional admission

    If it's a fit, we review the house agreement, confirm her appointments, and set a move-in date.

  4. 4

    Stay in the loop

    With her written consent, we keep the referring provider updated on how she's doing.

Our Commitments

What she, and you, can count on.

  • Safety first

    Naloxone on site and staff trained to use it. No woman is ever discharged for calling 911.

  • Return to use

    A return to use is met with a plan, not an automatic discharge. We don't move her until we know where she's going.

  • No cold exits

    If Roots & Wings turns out not to be the right fit, we plan a warm transfer to a better one, not a door.

  • No referral fees

    Aya does not pay or accept anything of value for a referral. Every woman pays the same published rate.
